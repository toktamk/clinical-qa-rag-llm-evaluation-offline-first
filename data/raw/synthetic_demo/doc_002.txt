Title: Risk Factors and Follow-Up in Condition Y

1. Background
Condition Y is associated with metabolic dysregulation and affects approximately 8% of the population.

2. Population
Adults with diagnosed Condition Y and at least one metabolic risk factor are included.

3. Intervention
Management includes lifestyle modification and optional use of Medication B.

4. Monitoring
Patients with Condition Y should undergo baseline cardiovascular risk assessment. Those with high-risk profiles require follow-up every 6 months, while low-risk patients may be monitored annually.

5. Outcomes
Improvement is defined as stabilisation of metabolic markers over a 6-month period.

6. Risks
Patients with high baseline risk factors have increased likelihood of adverse cardiovascular events.

7. Evidence Summary
Observational cohort studies involving 5,000 patients indicate that regular monitoring reduces adverse outcomes in high-risk populations.
