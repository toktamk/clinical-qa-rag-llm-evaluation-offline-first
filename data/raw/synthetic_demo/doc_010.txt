## **Title: Short-Term Stabilisation Protocol for Condition W Using Intervention J**

### **1. Background**

Condition W is an acute destabilising disorder affecting approximately 2–3% of the population. Rapid stabilisation is critical to prevent complications.

### **2. Population**

This guideline applies to adult patients aged 18–65 years presenting with acute episodes of Condition W. Chronic cases are excluded.

### **3. Intervention**

Intervention J is administered as an initial dose of 150 mg followed by a maintenance dose of 50 mg every 12 hours for 48 hours.

### **4. Monitoring**

Baseline assessment must include vital signs and symptom severity scoring. Patients must be monitored continuously for the first 6 hours.

Follow-up monitoring must occur at 12 hours and 24 hours. Routine monitoring may be discontinued after 72 hours if stability is achieved.

**Conditional monitoring rule:**
If instability persists beyond 24 hours, monitoring must continue every 6 hours until stabilisation.

### **5. Outcomes**

Successful stabilisation is defined as symptom resolution within 24–48 hours. Failure is defined as persistent instability after 72 hours.

### **6. Risks**

Common adverse effects include sedation (22%) and hypotension (10%). Severe complications occur in approximately 1% of cases.

### **7. Evidence Summary**

Evidence from controlled trials involving 900 patients demonstrates rapid stabilisation with Intervention J compared to standard care.
