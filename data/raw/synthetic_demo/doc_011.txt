Title: Conflicting Evidence on the Effectiveness of Therapy K in Condition M
1. Background

Condition M affects approximately 5–8% of adults. Therapy K is widely used, but its effectiveness remains debated.

2. Population

Applies to adult patients aged 18–70 years with confirmed Condition M.

Different studies use different populations, including:

general adult population
high-risk subgroups
patients with severe baseline symptoms
3. Intervention

Therapy K is administered at 50 mg daily for 12 weeks.

4. Monitoring

Baseline and follow-up at 6 weeks and 12 weeks are recommended.

5. Outcomes

Study A reports a 30% improvement in symptom severity at 12 weeks in the general population.
Study B reports no statistically significant improvement in high-risk patients.

Study C reports improvement in laboratory biomarkers but no improvement in patient-reported symptoms.

Study D reports delayed improvement only after 24 weeks, which differs from other studies.

6. Risks

Adverse effects include fatigue (15%) and headache (10%).

7. Evidence Summary

Evidence is inconsistent due to:

different populations (general vs high-risk)
different endpoints (symptoms vs biomarkers)
different timeframes (12 weeks vs 24 weeks)
