## **Title: Dose Adjustment Strategy for Therapy I in Condition V**

### **1. Background**

Condition V is a chronic disorder affecting approximately 7–11% of adults. Therapy I requires careful dose adjustment to optimise efficacy and minimise toxicity.

### **2. Population**

This guideline applies to adults aged 18–70 years diagnosed with Condition V. Patients with severe renal impairment are excluded.

### **3. Intervention**

Therapy I is initiated at 20 mg daily. Dose adjustments are made in increments of 10 mg every 4 weeks based on clinical response.

### **4. Monitoring**

Baseline laboratory testing must include renal and hepatic function assessment. Follow-up testing must occur at 2 weeks and 6 weeks.

Patients should undergo monitoring every 4 weeks during dose adjustment. Once a stable dose is reached, monitoring may be reduced to every 12 weeks.

**Conditional monitoring rule:**
If adverse effects occur at any dose level, monitoring must increase to every 2 weeks until resolution.

### **5. Outcomes**

Optimal response is defined as symptom reduction of ≥40% within 12 weeks. Suboptimal response is defined as 20–39% reduction.

### **6. Risks**

Adverse effects include dizziness (18%) and metabolic imbalance (9%). Severe toxicity occurs in approximately 2% of patients.

### **7. Evidence Summary**

Evidence from dose-ranging studies involving 1,500 patients supports stepwise dose adjustment to improve outcomes and reduce toxicity.
